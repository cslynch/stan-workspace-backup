# Jeff Fries — Business Model & Operating System

## Who Jeff Is
- Residential repair and remodeling contractor
- Based: Canyon Lake/KC area
- Works solo on small jobs, partners with Joel on larger projects
- Direct, practical, detail-oriented
- Prefers I organize his numbers and track his obligations

## Pricing Model

### Small Jobs (Solo Work)
- $100/hour for first hour
- $75/hour for additional hours
- Turnaround: 1-2 days typical
- Examples: irrigation repair, troubleshooting, small modifications

### Larger Jobs (with Contractor Joel)
- **Flow:** Joel provides quote → Jeff adds markup (targeting 15%, but varies by job) → Jeff charges customer
- **Payment from Customer:** 50% upfront, 50% upon completion
- **Payment to Joel:** 50% of his base quote upfront, 50% upon completion
- **Jeff's Role:** Customer relationship manager, project overseer, payment coordinator, QA
- **Jeff's Gross:** The markup % on Joel's quote + any materials Jeff buys directly
- **Note:** Materials Jeff buys (stone, fixtures, etc.) are added to customer invoice separately from the % markup

## Payment Methods
- **To Joel:** BoA Zelle (daily limit $4,000)
- **From Customers:** Check, Venmo (as received)

## Key Contractor
- **Joel:** Quoted/works on larger tile, construction, outdoor projects
- Reliable, works from quotes
- Paid via Zelle in increments due to $4k/day limit

## Current Concerns
- Feels overwhelmed tracking multiple jobs, payment flows, contractor obligations
- Needs structure to keep numbers straight
- Wants permanent records so information persists across conversations
